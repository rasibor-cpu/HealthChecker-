ROOT_CAUSE=
Two cooperating defects made Heart Rate → Filtered Timeline show Steps, Sleep,
and Oxygen Saturation even though UAT12G compaction itself was correct.

1. FILTER PROPAGATION / DOUBLE LOAD
   health_snapshot.js Filtered Timeline → HCConsumerSurfaces.openFiltered()
   set metricFilter and called loadTimeline({metric:heart_rate}), but also
   tab.click() on Timeline. index.html onclick only switches screens, yet
   consumer_surfaces bind() SCREEN_LOADERS fire on the same click and call
   loadTimeline() with no options. That second GET is
   /api/health-vault/timeline?unified=true (no metric). On S24 that unfiltered
   response can complete after the Heart Rate request and overwrite the UI
   with compacted Steps / Sleep / SpO2 / Heart Rate cards.

2. SERVER FILTER OVER-MATCH
   timeline_entry_matches_metric() treated the short alias "hr" as a substring
   of summary/trend text. Sleep rows containing "hours" could pass
   metric=heart_rate. Tightened to measurement-token match plus phrases of
   length >= 4 only.
