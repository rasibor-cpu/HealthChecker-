Symptom Checker (Expanded)

Add up to 10 symptoms. This is a triage-style engine. It weights risk using age, conditions, and red-flag patterns (stroke, heart, bleeding, sepsis cues).
Emergency red flags include: one-sided weakness, facial droop, speech difficulty, severe chest pain, severe breathlessness, black/tarry stool, vomiting blood, fainting, new confusion. Seek urgent care.
