Instructions

1. Start from the Profile tab. Enter your details and add any existing conditions (from suggested dropdowns or your own text). This helps the engine weigh severity.
2. Use the Symptom Checker to add up to 10 symptoms. For each one, select a body part, pick a common symptom from the dropdown, adjust severity (1–10) and duration, and optionally type any extra notes. Click Get Diagnosis for a general triage-style recommendation.
3. Use Urine, Stool and Heart / Hydration helpers for focused checks. Each generates a severity rating and recommended action and updates your Dashboard & Reports.
4. In Payments, record single bundles or subscriptions:
• ₦200 = 3 diagnosis credits
• ₦500 daily
• ₦2,499 weekly
• ₦9,999 monthly
• ₦79,999 annual
Every 10 paid credits earns a bonus free diagnosis. These unlock report export actions.
5. The Dashboard provides a triage-style overall risk snapshot (Low / Moderate / High) based on recent checks and your conditions. This is a general tool only and does not replace your doctor.
